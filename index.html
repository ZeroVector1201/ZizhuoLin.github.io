EMNLP 2026 Main · Accepted

ToM-Video: A Theory-of-Mind Video Benchmark with Spatio-Temporal Grounded Reasoning

Zizhuo Lin · Co-first author

Understanding beliefs and intentions in video through explicit spatial and temporal evidence.

Method & results

ToM-Video includes 1,487 source videos, 4,666 clips, and approximately 41,000 question–answer pairs, with timestamps, key-frame bounding boxes, and evidence chains. INSPIRE combines supervised fine-tuning with grounding and theory-of-mind reinforcement learning.

Qwen3-VL-8B achieves 76.2% on the ToM-Video validation set, a 5.4 percentage-point improvement over the SFT stage. Social-IQ 2.0 accuracy improves from 63.41% to 65.12%.

Under review

What Shared Prefixes Hide: Trajectory Dropout for On-Policy Distillation

Zizhuo Lin · First author

Strengthening token-level supervision by selectively masking the student’s shared reasoning prefix.

Method & results

Trajectory Dropout addresses weak supervision caused by highly confident predictions and teacher–student agreement on shared prefixes. It masks 20% of the student’s historical segments during training while retaining the teacher’s full context.

With Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 as teacher and Qwen3-1.7B as student in non-thinking mode, average accuracy across six mathematical benchmarks improves from 28.27% to 32.78% over OPD; GPQA Diamond improves from 31.57% to 35.73%.

Under review

Same Evidence, Different Answers: Canonical-Context On-Policy Distillation for Multi-Turn Language Models

Zizhuo Lin · First author

Improving answer consistency when the same evidence is presented across multiple conversation turns.

Method & results

CCOPD uses a frozen model with the complete context to supervise a multi-turn student through token-level reverse-KL distillation. Qwen3-8B is trained with LoRA on 6,000 paired mathematical conversations.

Multi-turn mathematical accuracy improves from 66.0% to 82.5%, while full-context accuracy remains at 90.3%. With mathematics-only training, the average score across five non-mathematical out-of-domain tasks improves from 36.8 to 49.7.
